Leer la descripción completa

NGL Biathlon 2027 is a biathlon simulator. Race as your favourite athlete and fight for the Big Crystal Globe

  • Athletes and tracks: a large roster of athletes, 15 tracks and every biathlon discipline
  • Career: many seasons, from a junior in the lowest league to the fight for the globe
  • Your own tournaments: you choose the tracks and races
  • Your own athlete: face, suit, skis, rifle and other gear
  • Online: real players, rooms of up to 21, ratings and tournaments
  • Broadcast mode: the race runs by itself while you switch cameras
  • Atmosphere: spectators along the course, finish-line celebrations and award ceremonies

We put serious work into the terrain and physics, so athletes now move along the course far more realistically. Tactics and pacing matter even more than before, while the game balance has stayed the same

Get behind a rival and you ski at their pace while saving energy, then spend what you saved on a surge closer to the finish. Bots do the same: they tuck in behind you too, and you can shake them off with a boost

We skied every track ourselves, and the recorded path became the bots' line. They take the short way through corners, hold their rows, overtake and decide for themselves when to speed up by watching their energy

If you are clearly in the lead, you can take a flag from a fan on the last lap and carry it to the finish. Bots do it too, and in career mode the flag earns you money

We redrew the menus and race screens from scratch: tables, banners, the start list

Soldier Hollow in the USA and Canmore in Canada

The season follows a World Cup calendar. Rivals now improve during the season along with you. Relays are no longer just for the World Cup, the lower leagues have them too, and the stats show your season in detail

At night, floodlights light up the course and stadium, and the light on the snow comes from the actual lamps. Snow and fog can be normal or heavy, with five weather options in all

Optional in the settings: realistic time display and a 30-second start interval
