Leer la descripción completa

Immerse yourself in a world of long highways and endless horizons in TRUCK SIMULATOR US DRIVER, where every kilometer is part of your journey. Feel the life of a real truck driver as you navigate through small towns, bustling cities, and quiet stretches of highway.

🌎 Explore a vibrant open world

Roam freely through diverse regions, park your truck, wander around, and discover new places at your own pace. Every road leads to a new opportunity.

📦 Complete missions

Choose tasks, plan routes, and decide whether to drive during the day or at night. Time and decisions impact every delivery.

⛽ Manage your survival

Keep an eye on your fuel, rest, food, and truck health. Breakdowns, flat tires, and unexpected problems can turn a simple trip into a real challenge.

🏪 Live the life of a driver

Get out of your truck to refuel, buy food, rest, and prepare for your next trip. The road doesn't end when you leave the driver's seat.

🚚 Grow and customize your truck

Upgrade your trucks, expand your fleet, and increase efficiency as your career progresses.

In Truck Simulator US Driver, the road is more than just a destination, it's a way of life.

Start your engine. Choose your route. Drive your story in TRUCK SIMULATOR US DRIVER!
