Leer la descripción completa

Rekindled is a co-op tavern management game where you run your own tavern in a fantasy-medieval setting. You'll gather ingredients by foraging, fishing and farming, cook them in a variety of cooking stations, and present them how you'd like on plates for your customers. You can use the money earned to expand your tavern, buy more decorations, adopt pets and much more.

Rekindled is different to most cooking games - there's no stress! Customers don't make any demands of you; they simply buy the dishes you craft and arrange yourself.

Exploration

The area around your tavern is rich with resources and secrets, and you're free to explore as much or as little as you'd like.

Decoration

Expand your tavern and unlock, build and purchase a huge variety of furnishings to decorate it with. There is a silly amount of freedom (you can place items on walls and ceilings if you really want to!) so you can truly make the tavern your own.

Cooking

Enjoy total freedom to create the dishes you want to offer! You can make cheese boards, dessert trays, sushi platters and loads more, in any combination your heart desires. Unlock new foods and creation stations to unleash even more creative options.

Co-op Multiplayer

The game can be played perfectly on your own, and you can also invite an un-capped number of your friends to help out. You share the same unlimited inventory for maximum freedom, and can collaborate or chill out as much as you want.

Seasons and Weather

Enjoy a variety of weather across the four seasons including fog, rain and snow. There's nothing cosier than hunkering down in your tavern during a thunder storm!

Guests will react to the weather as well, so you may want to cover any tables you placed outside!
