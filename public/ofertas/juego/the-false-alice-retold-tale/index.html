◆ Story

“When Failed Creations with the same name meet, they have to kill each other.”

This is the Remnant World, a place where Failed Creations from abandoned stories are gathered.

Failed Creations who share the same name must kill one another, and only the last one left alive can become the “real” one.

In this world governed by that cruel rule, an amnesiac Sensei awakens.

There, Sensei encounters three Alices, each a Failed Creation of Alice in Wonderland.

The three of them are likewise destined to kill one another in order to become the “real” Alice.

However, their encounter with Sensei sends that destiny wildly off course.

This is a retold tale of three Alices and an amnesiac Sensei setting out on a journey together...

■ Characters

・Prima, the red Alice

VA: Aoi Yuki

A Failed Creation of Alice.

Clad in a red dress and armed with a single blade, she acts on impulse and instinct.

Reckless and prone to acting before she speaks, she nevertheless cares deeply for her companions. Her trust in and attachment to Sensei are especially strong.

Though she occasionally displays a dangerously possessive side, a little praise is all it takes to cheer her up. She is a pure-hearted and straightforward Alice.

・Secunda, the Alice with the bow

VA: Chiwa Saito

A Failed Creation of Alice.

A calm and collected archer who excels at ranged attacks, reconnaissance, and assassination.

Though sharp-tongued and aloof, she is attentive to the needs of others. She recognizes Sensei’s abilities as a strategist and offers her cooperation.

She is not very good at hiding her embarrassment, making the sincere side she occasionally reveals all the more memorable.

・Tertia, the Alice with the hat

VA: Misato Fukuen

A Failed Creation of Alice.

A white-hatted sorcerer who wields magic.

Though timid and somewhat clumsy, she has the determination to make the decisive move when it matters most. More than anyone, she wants to place her trust in Sensei.

She is a kindhearted Alice who turns each small act of courage into a great victory.

・Alter Alice

VA: Inori Minase

A Failed Creation who closely resembles the “real Alice.”

She possesses overwhelming power, yet shows a puzzling kindness and hesitation only in Sensei’s presence, revealing two starkly different sides to her character.

As the embodiment of both the desire to become “real” and the laws governing this world, she is an unavoidable rival.

・Sensei

VA: Mariya Ise

The protagonist of this story.

Having lost all memories, Sensei sets out on a journey with the Alices.
