Leer la descripción completa

Welcome to Cloys Inc., the universe's leading toaster company, and your new family! You've been put in charge of cleansing Kalahari of its radiation. Harvest resources, import Cloys to work for you, and explore this mysterious alien planet to unearth the nodes of your upgrade tree.

The Best Toaster In The Universe​

In order to do your work, your employer thankfully provides you with Cloys, high-tech toasters able to toast bread, make phone calls and mine materials in hazardous conditions. Choose whether you want to play fully active, or idle while your Cloys work automatically.

Harvest all kinds of resources, improve your clicks and your Cloys, build new structures, and harvest more resources! The pace is steady, and the numbers remain understandable.

Explore An Alien Planet​

The planet you've been assigned to cleanse isn't just an empty field. Some weird structures stand, hidden between towering mountains, in the middle of acid lakes or behind mysterious doors... Explore its nooks and crannies in order to become always more efficient at your job!

The nodes of your upgrade tree are hidden throughout the world, and unearthing them as you explore further will make your task a lot easier! Lift the fog of radiation shrouding the planet and uncover pulsating fields, monoliths and other oddities. Study them, repair them, build them, upgrade them and become a true harvesting machine!

Restart stronger, faster

Once you've accumulated a big enough payout during a session, end it! Upgrade your permanent outposts, let the world rest, and start a new session to earn an even bigger payout!

Each new session will take you further, to discover new upgrades and resources. It also makes management happy, so...
