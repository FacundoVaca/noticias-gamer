    Experience a new twist on a familiar genre 🔥

    Astral Throne II: Age of the Phoenix is a Roguelite Strategy/Tactical RPG, blending elements of the classics with a modern twist.

    Embark on a fantastical journey of revenge and conspiracy, written by award-winning fantasy novelist Shami Stovall , writer of series such as Firth Chronicles , Chronos Chronicles , and more.

    Fight through a series of tightly designed strategic battles while building a formidable army though Roguelike-style upgrades and loot. Experiment with party synergies, combining skills, equipment, and classes as you advance across a procedurally generated world map.

    Unlock characters and grow their relationships at camp in-between runs. Customize your expeditions as you make lasting progress on your continent-spanning quest.

    Astral Throne II continues the unique blend of Roguelike, Strategy, and RPG introduced in its predecessor

    Strategize around Weapon and Magic triangles , stat and experience distribution, and permadeath

    Build each character throughout runs with loot, level-ups, shops , and other discoveries

    Encounters are handcrafted to retain the tight design of a strategic experience, but have pockets of variation to keep you on your toes

    Strategically navigate a dynamic ever-changing world map , ensuring no two runs look alike

    Build a varied team of memorable characters , from valiant knights to wily gamblers and everything in between

    Synergize your builds with hundreds of skills, weapons, and items , which differ every run

    Adapt your gameplay to run-altering collectibles that can significantly shift gameplay mechanics

    No grinding or stat-increasing meta-progression, you must strategize to win

    Experience a deep and compelling narrative written by the award-winning fantasy novelist Shami Stovall.

    Break free from prison and progress through a continent-spanning quest of revenge and conspiracy

    Make long-term meta-progress as you complete each act piece by piece

    Deepen your character's bonds through a relationship system that progresses with each run (and can even turn romantic , if you choose)
