Double Whammy

Double Whammy is a genre-bending shop-management / action game that follows Gigi, an aspiring guitarist, as she balances her day job at a guitar shop with nightly battles in the dream realm. 🎸🎸

Story

After discovering an old guitar instruction book, Gigi opens a portal to the dream realm filled with otherworldly threats. Although each night's trial ends at sunrise, her guitars remain damaged in real life. To survive the next battle, she must work at the guitar shop to repair her broken guitars and acquire new gear.

Six-String Playground

Design wild custom guitars from scratch - shape them, paint them, and take them into battle or jam with them in the rhythm game.

Tune, polish, and repair guitars in mini-games at the guitar shop. Serve customers, earn money, and prepare your guitar loadout.

At night time, grab your favorite guitar and dive into fast-paced dream realm combat. Smash and shred through monsters!
