Embark on a brand new adventure in Melvor Idle 2!

Melvor Idle 2 expands upon the beloved foundations of its predecessor and the wider world, to bring a deeper, richer, and more refined idle gaming experience. This single-player adventure lets you explore an ever-growing world of skills, combat, and strategy at your own pace.

Whether you're a veteran of idle games, a fan of the original Melvor or a maxxed out RuneScape completionist, Melvor Idle 2 offers a fresh adventure that balances depth with accessibility. It's the ultimate idle RPG for both busy adventurers and those ready to immerse themselves in a constantly evolving world

Master Melvor’s many unique skills with just a click or a tap. Melvor Idle 2 is a feature-rich, idle/incremental game combining a distinctly RuneScape feel with a fresh gameplay experience. Maxing your skills has never been more zen! If you are looking for a deep but accessible adventure that easily fits around a busy lifestyle, then Melvor Idle 2 is an idle experience unlike any other.

Every skill in this game serves a purpose, interacting with the others in interesting ways. This means all the hard work you put into one skill will in turn benefit others. What strategy will you conjure to achieve maximum skillage?

It doesn’t just end with Woodcutting, Smithing, Cooking and Farming either – take your finely honed tapping abilities into battle and face off against monsters using your Melee, Ranged and Magic skills. Conquering brutal dungeons and toppling boisterous bosses has never been quite like this before…

In addition to the skills to level up, there’s Quests, offline progression, cloud save compatibility, an integrated Bank/Inventory system and more! And thanks to its regular updates, the adventure continues to grow all the time.

Do you have what it takes to conquer Melvor?

A Brand-New Look and Feel: Experience Melvor like never before with updated graphics , enhanced visuals, animations, audio, and a modernized user interface, all designed to make your adventure even more immersive and engaging.

New Skills: Build upon the legacy Skills from the original game with newly introduced Skills , adding greater variety and synergies to your journey.

Quests: Embark on engaging story-driven quests that uncover the rich history of Melvor that guide you through your journey. You will face powerful Quest Bosses, train unique Skilling content, and help save Melvor from devastation along the way.

Day/Night Cycle & Weather: Enjoy a more immersive experience with a customisable dynamic day/night cycle & weather system that adds visual flavour and atmosphere to your adventure.

Brand New Combat Mechanics: A familiar Combat system, now updated with new mechanics that increase strategic decision making.

Mastery Guilds - Dive into the Master Guilds , a completely reimagined Mastery system for all skills, including Combat.

Current Action Tracking: See all information about your hourly rates based on your current action. This includes Skill XP/hr, items used & gained/hr, Combat stats/hr and everything in between.

Event Log: Tired of missing notifications for milestones, items, offline progression breakdown etc? You’ll now be able to view all notable events that occurred during your session.

Updated Bank & Equipment Management: Placeholders, keyboard shortcuts, item tagging, Bank Tab names, Equipment set names, updated equipping menu from Combat itself, and more is being introduced to streamline this overwhelming area of the game.

and so much more!

Maximum Level 99: Push your Skills to thhe limit with a maximum level of 99 .

Single-Player Gameplay: Enjoy an adventure crafted for you to play at your own pace.

Built for Mods: Designed from the ground up to support user-created Mods in the future.

Offline Progression: Continue progressing while offline at the same speed as when actively playing, ensuring you never miss out on growth.

In-Depth Skill Progression: Master 25 Skills with meaningful mechanics and interactions.

Dynamic Combat System: Take on monsters, formidable bosses, and epic dungeons with new & refined Combat mechanics.

Robust Inventory System: Effortlessly manage your items with an improved Bank & Equipment system.

Cross-Platform Cloud Saving: Seamlessly play on PC and mobile (when available) with integrated cloud-saving functionality.

Regular Expansions: Experience an ever-growing world with frequent updates and new content, including DLCs that will increase the level-cap and more.

Basic Social Features: See how many other players around the world are training the same thing as you with our Presence feature.

Plus familiar features such as Pets and more!

For Melvor Idle 2 , we believe in fair and enjoyable gameplay for everyone. That’s why the game contains no microtransactions . Every player experiences the full base game without needing to spend extra money, ensuring a level playing field where progress and success are earned purely through your efforts.
