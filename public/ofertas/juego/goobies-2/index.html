Leer la descripción completa

The goobies are working together with the blobs! Gaia has assembled the most powerful goobies in order to stop Haibel's mind controlling tyranny and bring back a forgotten peace...

Goobies come in all shapes and forms, each with unique abilities and evolution trees. Choose from the original 5 goobies or discover the 5 new ones!

Design your goobie by unlocking and combining more than 60 genetic goobie mutations. Each mutation behaves differently and can be added multiple times into the pool as long as there is space.

Gather strength while you can by collecting over 40 powerful omens that evolve with new properties as they grow in quantity.

36 artifacts and 3 difficulties await you to alter the rules of the blobverse...
