Leer la descripción completa

A short incremental game where you must purge a derelict research facility overrun by creeping fleshy growths. Armed with your flamethrower , you'll burn through spreading biohazards, uncover secrets and grow ever more powerful as you progress.

Collect the blood dropped by these organic growths and spend it in a vast upgrade tree to unlock new abilities to change the course of your runs.

As you clear rooms and progress through floors, uncover the eerie secrets behind the research facility and unravel the mystery of what really happened to you.

Explore and clear all five levels to contain the growth.
