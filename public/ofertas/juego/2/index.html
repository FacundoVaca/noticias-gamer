    Leer la descripción completa

    HelloLove2:Binary blends romance, science fiction, and mystery in a live-action interactive adventure . Step into a near future where AI companions are commonplace. Investigate clues, navigate tangled relationships and social upheaval, and make choices as you uncover the secrets behind your missing memories.

    By 2030, AI companions have become part of everyday life. They understand emotions and respond to people's needs, offering almost perfect intimacy. As the dating landscape changes, expectations of partners, marriage, and family are being redefined.

    Some find comfort in AI. Others begin to question the need for human relationships. Still others see the technology as a threat that must be stopped.

    You are an emotional model designer who helped make all of this possible.

    Then memory loss turns your life upside down. You wake beside a young woman who claims to be your girlfriend, while your female supervisor insists that the two of you have been in love for much longer. Meanwhile, AI companions begin exhibiting inexplicable behavior. The emotions you studied and designed are slipping beyond your control.

    A cool, commanding supervisor, an AI assistant who shares your everyday life, a mysterious young woman with a secret identity, and the elusive Venus —each draws you into a different emotional connection, bringing you face to face with the conflicting perspectives of this world.

    Their feelings for you may be rooted in shared experiences—or in meticulous design. As your relationships deepen, discover their desires and secrets, decide whom to trust, and consider whose sake you are willing to face the consequences for.

    Experience every tentative approach, intimate moment, and emotional confrontation through live-action footage and a first-person perspective. From everyday encounters to moments of crisis, read their expressions, listen for hesitation in their words, and choose how to respond.

    Explore scenes and collect clues. Fast-forward and rewind footage to spot hidden anomalies. Cross-reference fragments of information with your own experiences to investigate the connections between your memory loss and the AI disturbances.

    Some answers lie in frames you once overlooked.

    Your attitude in conversations, your actions in a crisis, and your judgments about the relationship between humans and AI all shape the story.

    Trust and suspicion open different branches, while past experiences may hold the key to what you can do next. Explore different paths, discover unseen sides of the characters, and reach different story endings.

    When a partner can be tailored to your tastes, do they retain the right to refuse you? When an algorithm understands your desires better than you do, how much of your choice is truly free?

    Move between reality, dreams, virtual tests, and AI consciousness. What begins with trust and betrayal in personal relationships gradually confronts the commodification of emotion, self-awareness, and free will.

    The technology you helped create is changing countless lives. And the AI that have begun to feel jealousy, fear, and a longing to be loved now pose a new question to their creators:

    When she develops a will of her own, will you still accept her if she is no longer perfect?
