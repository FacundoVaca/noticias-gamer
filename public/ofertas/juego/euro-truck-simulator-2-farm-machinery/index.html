Leer la descripción completa

Take a break from the big city and cruise into the countryside with the Farm Machinery DLC for Euro Truck Simulator 2! Deliver a range of specialized farming equipment through winding rural roads and golden fields. Enjoy the peaceful charm of farm life and take pride in helping European farming flourish! From powerful tractors to compact farm equipment, this pack offers a range of unique cargoes from two in-game brands - Owl and Greenler - ready to be hauled on low bed trailers to agricultural industries of all sizes across the landscapes of Europe.

This DLC contains the following cargoes:

- Autonomous Tractor

- Articulated Crawler Tactor

- Forage Harvester

- Grain Trailer

- Square Baler

- Disc Harrows

- Fertilizer Spreader

- Planter Sprayer

- Seeding Combinations

And don’t forget to decorate your truck cabin with these farming-themed accessories!

- Lunch Box

- Scarecrow

- Straw Hat

- Stuffed Autonomous Tractor

- Harvester Toy
