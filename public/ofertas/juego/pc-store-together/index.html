Leer la descripción completa

You and your friends pooled everything you had, signed the lease on an empty unit and hung a sign in the window. Bare shelves, one workbench, just enough cash to fill a single order.

Build it. Fix it. Sell it. Try not to argue over who's stuck on the register.

Every build is done by hand. Mount the CPU, seat the RAM, screw in the motherboard, close the panel and pray it posts.

Real part-by-part assembly: CPU, cooler, RAM, GPU, PSU, storage, fans.

Compatibility actually matters. Wrong socket, wrong slot, not enough watts, no boot

Thermals, wattage and performance are simulated, so a bad build is a bad build

Benchmark the finished rig and slap a price tag on it

Customers drag in machines that "just stopped working." No error message. No details. Sometimes no side panel.

Test components one at a time to find the faulty part

Repair, replace or upgrade, then bill accordingly

Deliver a working PC back to a customer who still insists it was fine before

Expand the shop, add workbenches, testing stations and display areas

Unlock better hardware tiers as your reputation grows

Decorate and lay out the store exactly how you want it

Turn a one-room repair shop into the biggest hardware store in the city

Full online co-op from the first minute. No solo-only career mode, no unlocking multiplayer later.

Play with up to 4 players in the same shop

Split the jobs: one on builds, one on repairs, one on the register, one "supervising"

Work the same PC together, hand parts back and forth, fix each other's mistakes

Historial de precios

Es nuestro propio registro: anotamos el precio de cada tienda todos los días que el juego está en oferta (los días sin oferta quedan en blanco). Empezamos a registrar el 22 de noviembre de 2026, así que todavía cubre 3 días como máximo. Los mínimos históricos de años anteriores vienen de IsThereAnyDeal y CheapShark, y se indica en cada caso.

Historial de precio en Steam

Seguimos el precio de PC Store Together en Steam desde el 24 de noviembre de 2026 (1 día de registro). En todos los días con oferta que registramos costó US$5.77.

Cómo calculamos el precio en pesos

Tomamos el precio de la tienda, lo pasamos con el dólar oficial del día (salvo Xbox, que ya está en pesos) y le sumamos IVA 21% y percepción 30%. Es una estimación: el precio final lo define tu banco.
