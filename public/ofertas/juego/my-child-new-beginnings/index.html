Leer la descripción completa

A new chance, a new school, and a new village. It's time for a new beginning with you and your child.

When things go badly for you and your adoptive child, Karin or Klaus, two years ago, you both move to a tiny apartment in a new village where you meet friendlier neighbors and try to start a new life. Life was challenging but you had each other, as you both moved on from the trauma you encountered. Now you have the opportunity to move into a proper home and Karin begins in a new school, but not everything is as it seems as your child is holding back about their past and its up to you to discover what it means to heal together.

This new emotional successor, inspired from the BAFTA award-winning, My Child Lebensborn, places you and your child in a new timeline and in a new setting where you both learn to heal in a life after trauma. You’ll discover why some bad memories don’t go away and encounter new challenges as Karin or Klaus deals with intense trauma responses brought on by the past.

My Child New Beginnings on Steam comes with over 25 chapters ready to play from start to finish. Your child will brave a new school, learn to make new friends, and finally take the first steps to confronting their traumatic past. As you grow with your child, you will explore topics of mental health and wellbeing and discover new ways to help calm and guide your child through the challenges of life.

The adventure won't end here either as we look forward to bringing new adventures in the years to come. You can even bring over your child that you raised in My Child Lebensborn and begin this new chapter with them!

The Steam version is packed with bonuses that you can't find on other platforms including the The Mountain and Recorder Reporter premium story packs. You'll also be able to explore different chapters and different ways to play through three save slots, and you'll have access to over 30 premium decorations right away to decorate nearly every room in your home!

Explore the stars and seek out the Aurora Borealis as you and your child hikes to the backcountry. Discover what it means for your child to be overwhelmed and why finding a quiet corner to yourselves can be a place of healing.

What happens when your child becomes an investigative journalist and discovers a surprising secret with the neighbors next door? Recorder Reporter discovers two unique chapters where your child learns the realities of responsibility and the emotions that come with it.

In addition to the dozens of decorations that can be purchased in the village shop, the Steam version of My Child New Beginnings comes with over 30 premium outfits and decorations to give your home a cozy touch.

Explore different outcomes and raise both Karin and Klaus! The steam version of My Child New Beginnings comes with three save file versions and you can even import your child from My Child Lebensborn as well!

Discover fun seasonal themes in our first decoration pack for Steam. The Seasons of Caring Decoration Pack sold separately is a huge bundle containing our most popular DLC packs released from our other platforms, including Frostmas Atmosphere, Hearts n Hugs, Back to School, and more! You'll even get the My Child Supporter Hoodie as thanks for helping support our game and our studios!
