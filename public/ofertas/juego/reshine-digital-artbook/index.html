Important note: Because typesetting and layout take an absurd amount of work, this artbook is available only in English .

Take a closer look at the creative journey behind Reshine . This 50+ page digital artbook brings together artwork, design explorations, and behind-the-scenes stories revealing how our tiny 3-person indie studio went from a hardcore city-builder to a cozy antique restoration game.

What’s Inside:

From Folklore to a Yellow Gear: How a shelved roguelite project and a three-hour sandblasting prototype unexpectedly became Reshine .

Crafting the Antiques: Narrowing 100+ historical objects down to 24, tackling a 60-piece "final boss" projector, and seeing what got cut.

Art & "Reverse" Texturing: Our 3D pipeline, the time-saving MEGA SCRIPT , and why we texture every antique backwards-starting clean and adding decades of grime.

Workshop, VFX & Tools: Designing a cozy rainy-day workspace (with a cat), symulating sandblasting physics, and breaking down every tool from the Sodablaster to the Laser.

Playtests & Trivia: Lessons from 8,679 playtesters, 1950s schoolbook inspired UI, and bizarre historical ads we found during research.

Tool Descriptions: A closer look at every restoration tool in your arsenal and the design decisions behind them.

How to locate the Artbook:

Right click on Reshine in your Steam Library

Go to Properties

Click on Installed Files

Click on Browse

You will see the Artbook in the Reshine folder
