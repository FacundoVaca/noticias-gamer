- Your hands rest, your dopamine works overtime!!

777 HERO is a pixel-art idle RPG that captures all the heart-pounding moments of pachinko!

And of course! It's built so that even people who know nothing about pachinko can have plenty of fun, so don't worry!

The controls are super simple too! There's absolutely no real-money gambling, and the only currency you'll ever spend is! The Souls you earn in the game!

- Simple controls!! Just leave it running until you hit the jackpot!!

Turn on Auto-fire and the balls launch all by themselves. Keep the window small and go do something else.

When you win, just hold down the Space bar. The Hero takes care of the rest. Easy controls!!!

Alert Mode: turns the music off and lets you know only when something good happens, with a sound and a flashing taskbar icon.

Mini Mode: keep a tiny screen tucked in the corner of your monitor and enjoy the true taste of an idle game!

Auto-saves whenever you quit. You can even quit in the middle of a RUSH and pick up right where you left off.

- All the flavor of pachinko, none of the gambling!!

If you hit the jackpot and the game congratulates you with a shrug, that's no fun at all, right..? So I've prepared all kinds of fun presentations!

Every draw is a fully independent roll with no hidden correction! The "Draws" counter even shows you, in hard data, just how unlucky your current losing streak is!

- But just leaving it running all the time gets boring, right!!

777 HERO isn't just a game where you wait around for the next draw! Pick your own win bonuses and power up your character!

Win in a row during RUSH mode and you get even more chances to choose!

- Win RUSH in a row, or hit 777, and a huge chance appears?!

Every few wins, a Jackpot Time begins where all kinds of rewards come pouring down! A chance to power up your character big time!!

Whatever lands in a hole is yours!! With a little luck, you can even get a high-rarity Gear Selection Box!

- Hit it and stuff explodes!!

Just swinging a sword would be boring! In this game, attacking a boss has a chance to set off all sorts of stuff!

There are many different ways to attack. Take on the bosses with special gear and skill combos!

- Skill nodes bursting with personality!! 59 nodes in total! Conquer the game with your very own build!!

Skills that only bump up numbers felt boring, so I made special skills instead!

Each direction on the tree pursues a different play style! Research your very own build!

Resetting your skills is pretty cheap. So it's totally fine if you pick the wrong one by mistake!

- Boxes get opened with a kick!! If an aura shows up, is it a high-rarity chance?!

Collect gear and relics full of personality!

128 kinds of gear and 37 relics are waiting for you!

- Got a good weapon? Then you've gotta enhance it!

Content that will pump out endless dopamine! Weapon Enhancement is here!

For balance reasons, only weapons can be enhanced for now! Gotta show the bosses a little mercy too..

Weapon Enhancement unlocks after you've progressed a bit into the game!

- Clear hidden achievements for generous rewards and skill points! They even give you permanent effects?!

239 achievements are waiting for you! (They'll be updated up to 333!) — Most of the conditions are secret!

11 Title ranks — earn titles with permanent effects based on how many achievements you've completed!

The Story

Two years ago, my older sister left home and never came back. One million sword drills, zero real combat experience..... A timid twenty-year-old swordswoman sets out on a journey carrying nothing but the pocket watch her sister gave her.

But... what's this? The watch starts talking to her. It turns out a goddess with the power to stop time was living inside that watch, and she nags. A LOT.... Ugh... Where will these two's adventure lead..?!

777 HERO is a tear-jerking one-person project. I'm even doing the marketing myself..

But that doesn't mean the updates are slow!! hehe
