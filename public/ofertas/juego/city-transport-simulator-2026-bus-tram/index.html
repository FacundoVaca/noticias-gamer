Leer la descripción completa

Step into the next generation of urban public transport with City Transport Simulator 2026 , a combined bus simulator and tram simulator set in a dynamic European-inspired city. Drive in CTS detailed buses and trams, manage your fleet, create routes and timetables, and build a public transport network that responds to your decisions.

City Transport Simulator 2026 is FREE for all previous CTS owners! Transfer your DLCs at no cost and enjoy them upgraded with all the new features, including dynamic weather, free roam mode, realistic ticketing, and much more.

City Transport Simulator 2026 introduces fully dynamic weather and seasonal changes, creating a living, evolving city environment:

Seasonal city transformation – trees, foliage, and cityscape adapt to spring, summer, autumn, and winter

Sunny, fog, rain, snow, or thunderstorms – dynamically changing throughout the day

Temperature changes affect vehicle heating and cooling systems

All vehicles, buses and trams, feature realistic heating, cooling, and window operations

Fully operational wipers respond to rain and adverse weather conditions

Every bus now includes a fully functional ticket machine:

Sell tickets to passengers directly

Accept both cash and contactless payments

Integrated pay table ensures correct change is given

Add an extra layer of realism to your public transport management

Explore the city like never before in the new free roam mode:

Walk as a pedestrian and board AI buses and trams as a passenger

Take control of vehicles at any time and switch dynamically between passenger, bus driver, and tram driver

Hand over vehicles at endpoints without breaking immersion

The new dynamic lighting system brings the city to life:

Realistic day/night cycle with atmospheric lighting

Enhanced interior and exterior lighting for all vehicles

Shadows, reflections, and ambient effects updated for maximum realism

Every vehicle features:

Precise real-world physics

Authentic engine and ambient sounds

Interactive cockpits with fully simulated controls

Keyboard, gamepad, joystick, and steering wheel support , with preconfigured profiles for popular wheels

Custom control mapping to play your way

Plan and optimize your public transport empire:

Line Editor and Timetable Generator let you create custom tram and bus lines in just a few clicks

Assign vehicles and let AI trams and buses follow your schedule in real time

Monitor and optimize as your network grows alongside the city’s needs

Career or Sandbox – Play Your Way

👨‍💼 Career Mode

Start small and grow your operation: transport passengers, maintain timetables, earn XP, unlock new vehicles and upgrades, and increase passenger satisfaction . Expand your network across the city and become a transport tycoon.

🎮 Sandbox Mode

No restrictions, full freedom: explore the city, drive any vehicle, and manage lines as you like. Play with up to 15 pre-made tram and bus lines or create your own.

Deep Customization

Name your operation, city, stations, and routes

Choose from multiple liveries, logos, and station designs

Customize branding on vehicles, buildings, signs, and announcements

Automated multi-language station announcements with through-line integration

Full control over station styles, routes, line colors, and naming

A Living, Breathing City

Drive across 50+ km of routes and 80+ unique stations in a city inspired by southern Germany. Enjoy a bustling open-world city center, scenic old towns, and quiet suburbs with a fully dynamic passenger system and licensed AI vehicles, including DHL vans, and familiar stores like REWE, Tegut, MediaMarkt, and Deutsche Post.

Play Your Way

Full simulation mode with every button and system realistically modeled

Easy mode for relaxed gameplay

Full input support: keyboard, mouse, gamepad, joystick, steering wheel (with many preconfigured setups)

Custom control layouts for every device

Long-Term Support & Expansion

City Transport Simulator 2026 is built for long-term support with ongoing updates, new vehicles, cities, and features. This is a living simulation that keeps evolving.
