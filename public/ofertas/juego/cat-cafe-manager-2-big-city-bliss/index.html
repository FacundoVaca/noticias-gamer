Leer la descripción completa

Build your cat cafe into the beating heart of the city, changing lives with cats, coffee, and community!

Return to the world of Cat Cafe Manager in this sequel to the hit cozy management sim.

After a faerie cat possesses your smartphone (it's a whole thing), you discover a mysterious force is leaving people lonely and disconnected. Rescue stray cats, grow your cafe, explore a folkloric city, and bring your community back together… one paw at a time. ✨

FEATURES

💗 Build your dream cat cafe

Create the coziest hotspot in town! Decorate to your taste, craft a menu with delicious treats, and match guests with cats to fill their cup—literally!

🐈 Rescue stray kittens

No two cats are the same. Get to know a cat's personality, earn their trust, and welcome them into your growing family.

😽 Care for your furry friends

Your kitties need food, grooming, playtime, and lots of cuddles to stay happy. Teach them unique companion skills to help you gather resources and uncover the secrets of the city.

🗺️ Explore a folkloric city

Become a local of Covestadt, a welcoming yet mysterious city with a touch of magic. Get to know colorful characters, uncover secrets and rare ingredients, and meet stray cats around every corner.

👋 Grow a thriving community

Meet a memorable cast of local shopkeepers, forge meaningful connections, and bring the bustling energy back to a once forgotten shopping street.

🧙‍♀️ Uncover an ancient mystery

Something ancient is stirring… Join forces with tiny cat faeries, uncover the witches' forgotten history, and save the city from a spreading loneliness.

✏️ A handcrafted 2.5D world

Inspired by Studio Ghibli and '90s shoujo anime, explore a handcrafted world where expressive 2D characters meet painterly 3D environments.
