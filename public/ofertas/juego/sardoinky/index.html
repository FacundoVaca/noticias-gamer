Leer la descripción completa

Sardoinky is a weird, chill, hand drawn clicker about opening cans of sardines. You work for a company that opens cans of sardines. That's it... That's the whole job.

Buy a can, SPAM THE BUTTON, peel it open and sell the nice fish inside. The cash buys more cans and upgrades. It's not really about the money, though. It's about the fish!!

You never know what's in a can until the lid comes off. Usually it's a sardine. Sometimes it's a foiled, stickered rarity worth a hundred times what you paid. Sometimes there's nothing in it at all.

As in any realistic gambling scenario, the odds are against you. Luckily, the company sells upgrades for that. Everything has a price. Even luck.

Sardines are just the start. There are weird fish, rare fish and a few things that have no business being in a can. Catch them all and fill out your collection.

What are you waiting for? Your shift starts now!
