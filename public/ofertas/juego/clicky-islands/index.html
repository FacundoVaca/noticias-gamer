Leer la descripción completa

In Clicky Islands, you play as a governor appointed by the King, and your mission is to find an island worthy of His Majesty’s future kingdom. Every island starts as a small village. Your goal is to upgrade it, stage by stage, until it’s big and developed enough to be called a Kingdom.

At first, you’ll do a lot of clicking, with recruitable units taking over the work as you grow. Later on, you’ll have to manage production chains and plan your islands to min-max your economy.

When you move on to a new island, your progress stays in the meta tree. Along the way, you’ll find ancient artifacts that you can mix and match to create a playstyle that suits you best.

Gather resources

Start by gathering basic resources with classic clicker gameplay, then recruit units to automate the grind. You'll need mountains of materials to upgrade your island.

Turn goods into gold

You will need a lot of gold to keep growing. Work out what each island is good for, build your production around it and ship your most valuable goods off to be sold.

Watch your settlement grow

Every new stage unlocks more buildings, more upgrades and new ways to push your production even further.

Explore the Meta Tree

During your voyages, you’ll earn reputation. Use it to unlock over 140 powerful perks in the meta tree. Every perk you unlock stays with you, so you start every new run stronger.

Dig up artifacts

Explore ancient ruins on each island to collect fossils. Between islands, break them open to uncover artifacts that change how you play. Like perks, they’re permanent and carry over to every run.
