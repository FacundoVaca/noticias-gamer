    Leer la descripción completa

    Hunt the undead, guard the secret of your origins, and unleash your true power where mortal weapons are no longer enough.

    Undead: Rise of the Betrayed King is a dark fantasy action RPG that takes you from battles on the ground to flight in dragon form. Explore forgotten places, fight with steel and flame, and find allies in a world consumed by dark magic.

    Your human guise lets you walk among people: speak with them, take on quests, and travel without revealing what you are. Yet beneath that shape lives an ancient dragon.

    Embrace your true form, spread your wings, and take to the skies. Unleash your fiery breath upon your enemies and continue the hunt from above.

    But fire cannot conquer everything. Some creatures can withstand even a dragon's flames. Facing them will demand a different approach.

    Your draconic power stays with you in barbarian form. Crush your foes with heavy weapon strikes and bring fire into the fight.

    Dodge incoming attacks, choose your position, and seize the moment to strike back. Within narrow crypts and ruined buildings, precise movement and combat skill will see you through.

    Undead, demons, and cursed knights stand in your way. Meet them with steel and dragonfire.

    Descend into grim crypts, cross shadowed forests, and explore ravaged settlements. Ancient ruins hold traces of a past that refuses to stay buried.

    Take on quests, uncover legends of dragons, and seek answers to the rise of the undead. Beyond the next turn may lie a forgotten secret — or the creature guarding it.

    In development: companion story campaigns, playable companions, and companion progression.

    Your companions will have stories of their own for you to experience through their eyes. Before a companion's personal campaign begins, they will accompany the main character, but you will not be able to take control of them.

    During their campaign, you will play as that companion and develop their abilities. Completing it will unlock the option to control them and continue their progression while they accompany the main character.

    Their personal stories will become part of your shared journey, turning familiar allies into playable characters.

    These features are not yet available in the current version. Character progression is planned for companions; the barbarian protagonist has no progression system.

    Undead: Rise of the Betrayed King is being built by a solo developer using Unreal Engine 5 and continues to grow.

    Thank you to everyone exploring this world, sharing feedback, and helping find bugs. Your support helps the game move forward.
